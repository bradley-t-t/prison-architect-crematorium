Description          "Deal with your dead on site. This adds a Crematorium and a futuristic Vaporizer, and guards carry the dead straight into them instead of leaving them for the hearse.\r\n\r\n[h2]How to use it[/h2]\r\n[olist]\r\n[*]Subscribe, turn the mod on in Extras > Mods, and load your prison.\r\n[*]Build a Crematorium ($1,500) or a Vaporizer ($3,000). They do the same job. The Crematorium smokes while it burns and the Vaporizer does not.\r\n[*]Zone the tiles under the machine and just in front of it as a Morgue. A Morgue can be outdoors, so the machine can stand in a yard.\r\n[*]Leave your Morgue Slabs where they are. A machine takes bodies from any slab within 25 tiles of it.\r\n[*]Unpause. Guards bring the dead in on their own, one at a time. Each body takes about a minute, with a short cool down after.\r\n[*]Build more machines if a riot leaves a queue. Every machine works on its own, and they can stand side by side.\r\n[/olist]\r\nThe tooltip shows how many bodies a machine has taken so far.\r\n\r\n[h2]How it works[/h2]\r\nThe game only ever carries a body to the nearest free morgue slab. Each machine hides two slabs inside itself, so a guard delivering a body walks it into the machine. The machine also takes bodies from your own slabs whenever it is free, so the queue never stalls.\r\n\r\n[h2]What changes in your game[/h2]\r\n[list]\r\n[*]While a machine is standing, no hearse comes. The game still sends one when a body is waiting, at most once an hour, and the mod turns it away at the edge of the map. The same goes for the hearse boat and helicopter on island maps.\r\n[*]A hearse that is already loaded finishes its run, so you never lose a body.\r\n[*]Remove every machine and the hearse works the way it always did.\r\n[*]With Robot Staff, Hauler drones fly the dead into the machines too.\r\n[/list]\r\n\r\n[h2]Conflicts[/h2]\r\nThe mod redefines the game's Morgue Slab, the Morgue room and the three hearse vehicles so it can attach scripts to them. Turn off any other mod that changes those."
